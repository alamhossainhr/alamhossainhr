I operate at the intersection of **Enterprise HRIS Engineering**, **Statutory Payroll Architecture**, and **Applied People Analytics**. With an interdisciplinary background bridging Botany (M.Sc.) and Human Resource Management (MBA), I develop automated enterprise workflows, predictive data frameworks, and custom software solutions to resolve plant operations bottlenecks and optimize workforce performance.

* 🔭 **I’m currently working on**: Enterprise HRIS engineering, statutory payroll automation, and workforce analytics applications.
* 👯 **I’m looking to collaborate on**: Full-stack web utilities (Flask / ASP.NET / SQL Server) and open-source data tools for HR operations.
* 🤝 **I’m looking for help with**: Advanced predictive modeling for people analytics and scalable cloud system deployments.
* 🌱 **I’m currently learning**: Deepening modern AI integrations for automated workflows and large-scale industrial data architecture.
* 💬 **Ask me about**: Bangladesh Labour Law compliance, statutory compensation matrices, database design, and productivity systems.
* ⚡ **Fun fact**: I hold graduate degrees spanning both Plant Biology and Human Resource Management—bridging natural systems with workforce dynamics!
